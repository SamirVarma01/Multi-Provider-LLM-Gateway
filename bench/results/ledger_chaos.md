# Ledger through crashes

Generated by `scripts/ledger_chaos` on 2026-11-09T14:02:13Z at commit `5b8bab0`. Host: Apple M2, Darwin; Docker VM: 8 CPUs, 3.8 GiB memory.

Load ran at 40 req/s from 4 keys for 142s, spread over three aliases: `bench` (OpenAI format), `failover` (OpenAI format, two targets, the first failing 10% of attempts before any output) and `bench-claude` (Anthropic format). Half the requests streamed, `max_tokens` ranged from 16 to 96, and half the streams asked for usage. Every mock response takes about a second (first token after 100ms, then 48 tokens at 50 tokens/s), so many requests are in flight at each kill.

The gateway's container was killed 20 times with SIGKILL, after running 3s–8s each time, and started again after 0s–2s. Load continued through the restarts and for 5s after the last one. The gateway ran with `REQUEST_TIMEOUT=20s` and `RECONCILE_GRACE=10s`, and its reconciler ran every 5s throughout. Once every reservation was older than the sum of the two, the test ran the reconciler until nothing was left open, then audited the ledger against the mock's records.
