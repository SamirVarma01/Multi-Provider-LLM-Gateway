Generated by `scripts/failover_test` on 2026-11-09T13:59:19Z at commit `5b8bab0`. Host: Apple M2, Darwin; Docker VM: 8 CPUs, 3.8 GiB memory.

The `failover` alias sends requests to `primary` first and fails over to `backup` (priority policy). Both are mock providers that answer after 50ms, then send 16 tokens at 100 tokens/s. Load is 50 req/s from one key, alternating streaming and non-streaming. After 10s of healthy load the primary starts failing; the fault lasts 30s, and load continues until the primary's breaker has closed again.

The gateway runs with its defaults: an attempt that has produced no output after `ATTEMPT_TTFT_TIMEOUT` (10s) is abandoned. A breaker opens after 5 failures in a row, or a failure rate of 50% over at least 5 attempts in 10s. It stays open 5s, then lets one probe through; a failed probe reopens it for twice as long, up to 60s, and 2 successful probes close it. Breaker states come from polling the gateway's `/metrics` every 50ms, so the times below are accurate to about that.

A request is counted in the phase in which it was **sent**. An error is any response that was not complete and well formed: an error status, a dropped connection, or a stream that ended without `[DONE]`.
